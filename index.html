An experimental in vivo investigation systematically evaluating five widely deployed Artemisinin-Based Combination Therapies (ACTs): Dihydroartemisinin-Piperaquine (DHA-PQP), Artemisinin-Piperaquine (ART-PQP), Artesunate-Sulfadoxine/Pyrimethamine (AS+SP), Artemether-Lumefantrine (AL), and Artesunate-Amodiaquine (AA).
