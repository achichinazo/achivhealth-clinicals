An experimental in vivo investigation systematically evaluating five widely deployed Artemisinin-Based Combination Therapies (ACTs): Dihydroartemisinin-Piperaquine (DHA-PQP), Artemisinin-Piperaquine (ART-PQP), Artesunate-Sulfadoxine/Pyrimethamine (AS+SP), Artemether-Lumefantrine (AL), and Artesunate-Amodiaquine (AA). Utilizing Modified Lowry microsomal protein normalization and Omura-Sato CO-difference spectroscopy, the study classifies net hepatic CYP450 inhibition and neutrality to guide safer polypharmacy in malaria-endemic populations.
